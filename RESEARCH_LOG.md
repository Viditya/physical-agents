## 2026-08-22

- **Topic:** Jim Fan (NVIDIA) — "Robotics' End Game" talk, Sequoia Capital AI Ascent 2026 (video: youtube.com/watch?v=3Y8aq_ofEVs). **Verified against the primary-source transcript** (Viditya downloaded the video + .srt; correcting the earlier same-day entry, which was based only on secondary write-ups).
- **Findings (verified from transcript):**
  - **"The great parallel"**: Fan's framing is that robotics should copy the LLM playbook — pre-train on world-state prediction (like next-token prediction), then action-fine-tune onto a "thin slice" of that simulation that matters for real robots, then let RL carry the last mile, then eventually physical auto-research (robots improving robots) — mirroring GPT-3 pre-training → InstructGPT SFT → o1 RL reasoning → auto-research.
  - **VLA → WAM**: VLA (vision-language-action) models — the category Gemini Robotics-ER 2 and Magma belong to — are called "LVAs" because most parameters go to language, making them good at nouns/knowledge but weak at physics/verbs (example cited: "move the Coke can to a picture of Taylor Swift" — generalizes to unseen images, but isn't real physical pre-training). Introduces "Dreamer," a World Action Model (WAM) that jointly predicts next world-state pixels AND next actions a few seconds ahead, zero-shot solving verbs never seen in training. **First "moment of silence" is for VLAs specifically** ("our dear friend VOS... long live World Action Models") — this is distinct from a second, later moment of silence for teleoperation.
  - **Data strategy ladder**: teleoperation (declining fast, ~3-24 hrs/robot/day ceiling) → wearable capture devices (UMI = wear the robot gripper/hand directly on a human hand; Dex-UMI = 5-finger exoskeleton) → egocentric human video at scale (EgoScale/"Ego-Exo": 21K hours of in-the-wild egocentric video, zero robot data, plus only 50 hrs mocap + 4 hrs teleop fine-tuning = <0.1% of the training mix) → future target: ~10M hours/year via an "FSD-equivalent" ambient data flywheel. Claims a genuine log-linear neural scaling law for dexterity vs. pre-training hours, echoing LLM scaling laws ~6 years later. **Second moment of silence is for teleoperation** ("long live sensorized human data").
  - **Environment/simulation scaling**: real-to-sim-to-real via iPhone 3D world-scans → classical physics simulator → "digital cousins" augmentation; then "Dream Dojo," a video-world-model-based neural simulator with **no physics equations or graphics engine at all** — takes continuous action input, outputs next RGB frame + sensor state, used to run massively parallel RL.
  - **Three remaining milestones** ("achievements on the tech tree"): (1) Physical Turing Test — indistinguishable human-vs-robot performance per unit energy/labor, Fan estimates ~2-3 years away; (2) Physical API — robot fleets configurable like software, enabling "lights-out factories" (design files in, assembled products out) and automated science "wet labs"; (3) Physical auto-research — robots autonomously designing/building better robots. Fan claims 95% confidence of reaching the end of this tech tree by 2040 (symmetric 14-year gap: AlexNet 2012 → today 2026 → 2040).
- **Correction to earlier same-day entry**: the claim "this critique doesn't apply to static-image extraction, per Fan's own framing" was **not something Fan says** — that scoping came from a secondary source's (Eventual.ai) interpretation, and Fan's talk never mentions static-image/document analysis at all; it's entirely about continuous embodied robot control. The narrow-scope argument for VMAN Vision (static extraction ≠ continuous physical manipulation) is a reasonable architectural inference we're making, not an endorsement from Fan — the pitch should be worded accordingly (our reasoning, not "even NVIDIA's Jim Fan agrees").
- **Why it matters for VMAN Vision**: still supports NOT pitching this as "M-Files builds/controls robots" (Fan's critique + the whole talk's momentum is squarely about embodied continuous control, an arena M-Files has no business being compared against) and keeping the pitch scoped to document/image understanding. But cite the reasoning as ours, not as something Fan explicitly validated.
