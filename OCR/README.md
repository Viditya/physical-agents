# Public engineering demo fixtures

These fixtures are selected for a general-purpose spatial-grounding
demonstration. Their source, licence, and retrieval details are recorded in
[`../fixtures/public-fixtures.json`](../fixtures/public-fixtures.json).

The P&ID PNG is a raster preview of a Wikimedia SVG asset. The original vector
source and the applicable licence remain linked in the manifest. The NASA
fixture is a PNG rendered from a declared Appendix A page of the source PDF,
which is retained under `fixtures/` with its source and presentation notice.

The public corpus also includes a declared LEVIA H7 sensor-schematic page
(CERN-OHL-S-2.0) and two Library of Congress HAER drawing sheets with their
individual "No known restrictions on publication" rights advisories. See the
manifest for exact source, notice, and attribution obligations.
